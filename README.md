# Mini Bike Speed Calculator

A specialized calculator built with Python and Streamlit to help mini bike and go-kart enthusiasts determine their top speed based on mechanical configurations.

##  How It Works
The app calculates top speed by analyzing the relationship between your engine's power and your drivetrain setup:
* Engine RPM: The speed at which your engine is spinning.
* Gearing: The ratio between your clutch/jackshaft sprocket and your rear sprocket.
* Tire Size: The height of your rear tire (which acts as the final gear).

## Features
* Live Calculations: Adjust sliders or inputs to see speed changes in real-time.
* Gear Ratio Breakdown: View your total reduction ratio to understand your torque vs. top speed trade-off.
* User-Friendly UI: Simple, clean interface designed for quick use in the garage or at the track.
